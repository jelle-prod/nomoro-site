Every kitchen has one. A bottle, a box, a tool that promised to make a routine easier and now asks for a routine of its own. You bought it to save a step. It added a drawer.

## The second task

This is what we mean by the second task. Not a flaw in any one product, but a pattern in how objects are designed and sold: the convenience is photographed, the maintenance is not.

## What this means for how we choose

Criterion 02 of the [Nomoro Standard](/standard/) exists for this reason. An object has to subtract more than it adds, and cleaning counts.
